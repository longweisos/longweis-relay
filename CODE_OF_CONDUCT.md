# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and focused on the auditable public client. Harassment,
personal attacks, discrimination, credential solicitation, and publication of
another person's private data are not acceptable.

Do not post credentials, customer content, private prompts, exploit payloads,
or private operational details. Report security concerns privately according to
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close contributions that violate these boundaries
to protect users and the project.
